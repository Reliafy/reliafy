This month's release is one big thing, **Reliafy now works inside your AI
agent**, and two that make it worth using: your existing diagrams come with
you, and repairable systems get exact answers.

## Use Reliafy from Claude and other AI agents

Reliafy is now an MCP server, the open standard AI agents use to call tools.
Connect it once and your agent can work with your reliability models,
datasets and block diagrams directly, using the same engine, conventions and
checks as the app.

**Connect in a minute**

- **claude.ai** (and the Claude desktop and mobile apps): Settings → Connectors
  → Add custom connector, enter `https://reliafy.com/mcp`, and sign in to Reliafy.
- **[Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code):**

  ```
  claude mcp add --transport http reliafy https://reliafy.com/mcp
  ```

- **Other MCP clients:** point them at `https://reliafy.com/mcp`. Setup and the
  full tool list are in the [API docs](/api-docs).

**Then just ask**

- "What's the reliability of my pump model at 2,000 hours, given it's already
  run 500?"
- "Build an RBD of this conveyor: a drive, two redundant gearboxes, and 2 out of
  3 rollers."
- "Now put a second motor in parallel with the first and re-run it."
- "Here's our BlockSim file. Import it and tell me which block limits
  availability."

**Built to be efficient for agents.** Your agent edits a diagram in place
instead of rebuilding it, and can hand Reliafy a file (a CSV, an Excel workbook
or a diagram from another tool) to import. Answers are exact wherever the maths
allows, and they say plainly when a value is estimated, a unit doesn't match or
a model is a placeholder. That means fewer tokens, fewer retries and fewer wrong
answers.

**Try it free, unlimited with Pro.** A free account gets 20 tool calls a month,
enough to see how it works on your own models and diagrams. Reliafy Pro
(US$19/month) removes the limit and adds fitting, simulation and fleet
forecasts from your agent. When you hit the limit, your agent can hand you an
upgrade link.

You can see and revoke your agent's access any time under Settings →
Connected apps.

## Bring your diagrams with you

Import a block diagram from **ReliaSoft BlockSim** (.rsgz / .rsr),
**Open-PSA** XML, a **Galileo** fault tree, or an **Excel** workbook, from the
Block diagrams page or through your agent. Every diagram also has a **Fault
tree** view. Excel import works for datasets and RCM worksheets too.

## Exact availability for repairable systems, free

Availability over time, over a mission and in the long run is now calculated
exactly rather than simulated, on every plan. It can start **from today**, with
some blocks already down or part-way through their life. Repairable diagrams
also take **repair crews** shared between blocks, **standby groups**,
condition-based and opportunistic **maintenance**, and safety functions with
**PFDavg and SIL**.

## Also new and improved

- **Design tab:** how many copies of each block give the most reliable system
  within a budget, or the cheapest way to reach a target, including
  common-cause groups.
- **Outage history:** load real up/down records and see the system's observed
  availability.
- **Demonstration test calculator:** how many units, for how long, to
  demonstrate a reliability at a given confidence.
- **Download any diagram as Python:** a standalone script that rebuilds the
  diagram with SurPyval and
  [RePyability](https://github.com/derrynknife/RePyability) and gives the same
  numbers. It's free on every plan.
- **Share an RBD with anyone:** read-only links for block diagrams, like
  models and datasets, now with an optional password and expiry.
- **Availability results you keep:** a simulation's result is saved with the
  diagram and knows when a change makes it out of date. Simulation is part of
  Pro (or purchased credits).
- **Optimal overhaul interval** for repairable systems fitted with Crow-AMSAA.
- **Start without a CSV:** paste values, enter known parameters, or open a sample.
- **Coming from Weibull++?** A "my censor column uses 1 = failed" checkbox.
- **Fleet alerts (Pro):** an email when a fleet's expected failures cross a
  threshold or jump.
- **Faster:** pages load only what they need, and the app starts quicker.
- **Fixes:**
  - Standby diagrams now calculate.
  - Charts size their time axis to the system.
  - Duane growth models report correctly.
  - The assistant models redundant equipment in parallel.
