How many spare final drives for next year? An illustrative haul fleet: 12 trucks, 24 final drives aged 1,500 to 19,000 hours, each running 500 hours a month, against a Weibull life (scale 25,000 hours, shape 2.2).

Reliafy's forecast, counting replacements that fail again: about 5.3 failures in 12 months, with a likely range (P10 to P90) of 3 to 8.

Stock 5 to cover the expected demand, 8 to cover nine years in ten. A constant-rate shortcut (fleet hours divided by the mean life) says 6.5: this fleet is still young, and the shortcut can't tell.
