Common-cause β matters where redundancy carries the function.

In our worked safety function, raising the 1oo2 transmitters' β from 5% to 10% moved PFDavg from 0.00448 to 0.00454. The single valve dominated. Add a second valve, put the pair in a group at β 10%, and PFDavg goes from 1.37e-4 to 5.70e-4: four times the independent figure.
