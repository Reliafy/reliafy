Reliafy now opens block diagrams and fault trees from BlockSim, Open-PSA, Galileo and Excel.

We checked the importer against the FFORT fault-tree benchmarks: every one of the 19 trees with an exact published answer matches, to at least 11 decimal places. Anything that can't come across exactly is listed, not guessed.
