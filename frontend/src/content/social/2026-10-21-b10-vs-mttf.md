B10 and MTTF answer different questions. Reliafy's sample pump model (a Weibull fitted to 18 failures and 7 pumps still running) gives a B10 of 9.5 months and an MTTF of 29 months.

The MTTF is an average, and 55% of these pumps fail before it. The B10 is when the first 10% have gone: build warranty, spares and inspection plans around that.
