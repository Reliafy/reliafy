How many wheel bearings for next year? Reliafy's sample fleet: eight delivery trucks, 500 hours a month each, against a fitted Weibull.

Expected failures over 12 months: about 40, counting replacements that fail again, with a likely range (80%) of 37 to 44. They won't arrive evenly: about 6.5 in month one, as the oldest bearings go, then 2.5 to 3.2 a month. Stock for month one, not the average.
