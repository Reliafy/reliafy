A proof test that can't find every failure costs more than it looks.

Our worked safety function, with its shutdown valve tested yearly, reaches PFDavg 0.00448: SIL 2. If each test finds only 70% of the valve's dangerous failures and a full test comes every 10 years, PFDavg is 0.0161. That's SIL 1, and the target is missed.
