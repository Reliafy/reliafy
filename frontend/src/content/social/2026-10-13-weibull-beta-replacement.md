Read β first. Below 1 means early failures: scheduled replacement swaps survivors for riskier new parts. Around 1 is random: age tells you nothing. Above 1 is wear-out, where preventive replacement can pay.

Reliafy's sample bearings fit β = 2.5. If a failure costs 7.5 times a planned swap, replacing at about 580 hours halves the long-run cost rate. At β = 1, the same calculation says run to failure.
