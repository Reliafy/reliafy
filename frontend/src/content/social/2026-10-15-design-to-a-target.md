Design to a target, not just against one. In Reliafy's Targets tab, the sample pump station (a controller feeding two redundant pumps) is 96.1% reliable at 200 hours. Ask for 99% and it allocates: the controller from 97.4% to 99.25%, each pump from 88.5% to 94.9%.

Hold the controller as it is and 99% can't be reached. It says so, and offers the best it can do: 97.374%.
