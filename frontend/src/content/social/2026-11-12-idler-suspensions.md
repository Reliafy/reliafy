Conveyor idlers changed at a shut while still turning aren't failures. They're suspensions: each one lasted at least that long.

An illustrative belt section: 20 idler sets, 6 failed in service, 14 changed at six-monthly shuts while still running. Fit a Weibull counting all 20 and the mean life is 41 months. Drop the 14 swaps, as a failures-only export does, and it's 23 months: 43% shorter, from the same idlers.

Plan around 23 months and you change sound idlers far too often.
