An illustrative phased mission: two engines (exponential, 100-hour mean life) and landing gear (Weibull, η 100 h, β 2). Take-off and landing, 0.3 of 5.3 hours, need both engines and the gear; cruise needs either engine.

Multiply each phase's reliability as if it started fresh: 99.2%. Reliafy's exact answer: 89.7%. Landing carries 96% of the risk: an engine lost in cruise doesn't end the cruise. It ends the landing.
