Did the new seal fix it? Before the change, a pump fleet had 40 seal failures in 100,000 operating hours. Say the new seal really halves the failure rate.

Run as long again, 100,000 hours, and there's a 70% chance the data shows it (at 95% confidence). For the usual 80% chance you need about 159,300 hours.

A 25% improvement is harder still: with only 40 failures before the change, no amount of running after it gives an 80% chance of seeing it. Work out the test before you claim the result.
