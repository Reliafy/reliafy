Availability tells you whether a plant is up. Production availability tells you what it delivered.

Reliafy's sample solar farm runs four 250 kW inverters against a 900 kW demand. In the long run it's up 99.87% of the time but delivers 99.60% of the demand: losing one inverter cuts output without stopping the farm. Over its first year, that's about 27,700 kWh lost.
