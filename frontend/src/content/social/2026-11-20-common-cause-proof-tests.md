Two transmitters voting 1oo2, each with a dangerous undetected failure rate of 5 × 10⁻⁶ per hour, proof-tested once a year. Tests take about 2 hours, repairs about 8.

Treat them as independent and both channels are down together about once in 540 years. Add a common-cause group with a β of 10% and it's once in 170 years: more than three times as often. The shared cause, not the pair, sets the number.

Reliafy's exact failure frequency now includes common-cause groups when proof tests take time.
