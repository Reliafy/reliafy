The Explorer's **Forecast…** and its **Spares for the year** have moved
(23 Nov 2026): a quick spares forecast is now a tool in the left menu.

On the Explorer's **Maintenance** tab, choose **Open in › Spares forecast** in a
position's, a task's or a work package's menu. **Decisions › Forecasts › Spares
forecast** opens filled in from that row and everything below it, and gives the
spares the units will use by period, with the likely range (90% two-sided),
**When next**, and how many to hold for the part's lead time at your service
level. Nothing is saved.

A part's own page still shows its stock, lead time and the orders placed.

See [Quick forecasts: spares, labour hours and costs](/guides/forecast-tools).
